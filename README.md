## 💛 A Letter to Parents: Why We Built TinyBloom

When our baby was born, we watched every doctor checkup with intense anxiety. 

At one pediatric visit, our doctor pointed to a graph and noted our baby was tracking along the lower curve. As first-time parents, our hearts sank. We spent sleepless nights questioning ourselves: *Was our baby eating enough? Were we doing something wrong? Why weren't they in the 80th or 90th percentile like the babies on social media?*

Then, our pediatrician gently held our hands and shared the truth that changed everything:

> **"Percentiles are not grades. They are not competitions or rankings.**
> A baby growing along the 15th percentile is just as healthy, capable, and thriving as one growing along the 85th percentile. Every baby comes into this world with their own unique genetics, their own natural tempo, and their own beautiful curve. All that matters is steady, consistent growth along their own trajectory."

We went home with a lighter heart and looked for a simple, calm app to track growth over time. 

Instead, we found commercial parenting apps packed with screaming red alerts, anxiety-inducing comparison bars, predatory $10–$15 monthly subscriptions, and aggressive data tracking selling private nursery moments to advertisers.

So we built **TinyBloom**. 

**TinyBloom belongs to parents.** No paywalls, no monthly fees, no accounts, no ads, and no surveillance. 100% of your baby's data stays securely on your phone.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 📸 1. Smart Screenshot Importer (Ditch Paid Subscriptions with Zero Data Loss)
- **No Paid APIs or Lock-in**: Commercial apps like Huckleberry, Sprout, Hatch, or Baby Tracker either lack export options or charge expensive fees.
- **Snap & Upload**: Simply take screenshots of your previous app's growth logs, historical charts, or doctor visit discharge papers.
- **AI Vision Extraction**: TinyBloom's smart vision engine reads dates, weights (kg/lbs/oz), heights/lengths, and head circumferences automatically.
- **Full Review Before Saving**: Review, edit, or adjust every extracted measurement before seamlessly adding it to your curves.
- **Ephemeral & Safe**: Uploaded screenshots are analyzed in memory purely to extract numbers, then immediately discarded—never saved to any database.

### 🛡️ 2. Absolute On-Device Privacy (Zero Cloud Tracking)
- **Local Sovereignty**: All measurements, photos, and milestone checklists reside in your device's browser sandbox storage (`localStorage`).
- **No Accounts or Passwords**: Never asks for your email, phone number, or password. There are no corporate servers that could ever be breached.
- **Zero Third-Party Telemetry**: Zero Facebook pixels, zero ad trackers, zero marketing analytics. Your nursery moments are not for sale.
- **Offline First**: Works completely offline—in pediatric clinics, on airplanes, or in the nursery without Wi-Fi.

### 📈 3. Clinical WHO Growth Percentiles
- **Official WHO Standards**: Calibrated to the World Health Organization Child Growth Standards (the gold standard recommended by the CDC and American Academy of Pediatrics).
- **Core Indicators**:
  - **Weight-for-Age** (kg / lbs·oz)
  - **Length-for-Age** (cm / inches)
  - **Head Circumference-for-Age** (cm / inches)
- **Interactive SVG Trajectory Curves**: Plots your baby's actual measurements against official 3rd, 15th, 50th, 85th, and 97th percentile bands.
- **Auto-Adapting Timeline**: Intelligently focuses the chart range based on your baby's current age (0–6m for newborns, 0–12m for infants, 0–24m for toddlers), with manual setting controls.
- **Reassuring Pediatric Context**: Explains what percentiles mean directly under the graph to keep anxiety away.

### 👶 4. Universal Milestones Guide (0 to 36 Months / 3 Years)
Granular, gentle developmental guidance covering every stage of early childhood:
- **0–1 Month (Newborn Cuddles)**: Chest-to-chest skin contact, high-contrast tracking, regaining birth weight.
- **2 Months (Social Smiles & Tracking)**: First intentional smiles, 45° head lifts, soothing coos.
- **4 Months (Chuckles & Head Steady)**: Belly laughs, rolling tummy-to-back, reaching for favorite rattles.
- **6 Months (Rolling & Grasping)**: Tripod sitting, single-ingredient solids, first teeth signs.
- **7–8 Months (Sitting & First Tastes)**: Independent sitting, pincer grasp, consonant babbles (`ba-ba`, `da-da`).
- **9–11 Months (Crawling & Waving)**: Hands-and-knees crawling, pulling to stand, waving bye-bye.
- **12 Months (First Steps & Stacking)**: First independent steps, first words, 1-year birthday milestone.
- **18 Months (Running & Talking)**: Word explosion, spoon feeding, running with joy.
- **24–36 Months (Stories & Curious Play)**: 2-to-3 word sentences, imaginative play, potty readiness, jumping with both feet.

## 📱 How to Install on Your Phone

TinyBloom is a certified Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it directly to your home screen with zero app store downloads:

### On Samsung / Android:
1. Open the app link in **Google Chrome** or **Samsung Internet**.
2. Tap the **Install** prompt in the header (or tap the `⋮` three dots &rarr; **Add to Home screen** / **Install App**).
3. TinyBloom will install into your phone's App Drawer with its own icon and launch full-screen like a native app.

### On iPhone (iOS):
1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**. TinyBloom will appear on your iPhone home screen with its gentle sprout icon.

## 🚀 Deployment Guide

### Option 1: GitHub Pages
1. Go to your repository on GitHub &rarr; **Settings** &rarr; **Pages** (in the left sidebar).
2. Under **Build and deployment** &rarr; **Source**, select **GitHub Actions**.
3. *(If your repository is Private on a free account, go to Settings &rarr; General &rarr; Danger Zone &rarr; Change repository visibility &rarr; Make public)*.
4. Go to the **Actions** tab &rarr; **Deploy TinyBloom to GitHub Pages** &rarr; click **Re-run all jobs**.
5. Your app will deploy to `https://<username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

### Option 2: Vercel
1. Connect your GitHub repository to Vercel.
2. Vercel will automatically use the included `vercel.json` and `.npmrc` (`legacy-peer-deps=true`).
3. Click **Deploy**.
